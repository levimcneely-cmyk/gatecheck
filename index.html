How the score works

Every landing gets rated across seven categories on a 0–10 scale, weighted by how much each one actually matters to a layover:

    Delay and rental-car situation start as multiple choice on the form, then get converted to the same 0–10 scale before the weights are applied. An airport's total is the weighted average of every visit logged there — so a bad day at a good airport won't tank it, but a pattern will.
